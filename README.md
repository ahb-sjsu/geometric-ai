## Part of the Geometric Series

This book is the crown jewel of the Geometric Series — the volume where every result from every other domain converges on the most important technical problem of the century: how do we build AI systems that respect the full geometric structure of human values?

It inherits the heuristic field formalism, geodesic deviation measure, failure taxonomy, gauge invariance diagnostics, and engineering toolkit from the parent text, and instantiates them on the value manifold — the multi-dimensional space where human values actually live.

**Central Claim:** Current AI alignment reduces multi-dimensional value structure to scalar objectives (reward scores, preference rankings, constitutional rules). The Reward Irrecoverability Theorem proves this approach is fundamentally broken. Geometric alignment — constraining AI systems on the full value manifold rather than scalar projections — is the mathematically necessary alternative.

**Foundation texts:**

- **Geometric Methods in Computational Modeling** (Bond, 2026a) — the mathematical toolkit
- **Geometric Reasoning: From Search to Manifolds** (Bond, 2026c) — the parent theory
- **Geometric Ethics: The Mathematical Structure of Moral Reasoning** (Bond, 2026b) — the moral manifold, No Escape Theorem, tensor-valued objectives
