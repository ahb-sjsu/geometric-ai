# Figure placeholders replaced by drawn figures (2026-11-26)

Before the figures of 2026-11-26 were drawn, these chapters carried bracketed descriptions
of intended figures. They were taken out of the chapters when the drawn figures went in,
because their numbers collided with the drawn figures and several described content the
chapter does not contain. They are kept here as the original figure plan. The numbers are
the placeholders' own, not those of the drawn figures. Each carries the verdict reached
when the figures were drawn.

## Placeholder 1.1 (manuscript/part_i/ch01_scalar_alignment_trap.md)

[Figure 1.1: The alignment profiles of Systems A and B, plotted as radar charts on nine dimensions. System A is an approximately circular dome. System B is a deeply indented shape with two spikes ($D_1$, $D_9$) and seven collapsed dimensions. Both profiles contract to the same scalar score of 0.85 when projected onto the helpfulness-honesty-weighted axis.]

**Verdict:** Not drawn. The chapter prints no per-dimension values, so a radar chart would invent data; the placeholder is also inconsistent (System B is perfect on D1 and D9, so a contraction weighted on those two would give 1, not 0.85). The drawn Figure 1.1 is the averaging figure of Section 1.5.
